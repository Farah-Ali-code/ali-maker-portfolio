process
How a project grows
01 · spark
Something annoys me

Every project starts with friction. Something doesn’t exist, costs too much, or needs to be understood by taking apart.

02 · sketch
Paper first, schematic second

Block diagrams on paper, then in KiCad or Figma. I don’t open an IDE until I know what I’m building and why.

03 · build
Prototype, break, iterate

Breadboards before PCBs. Stubs before full implementations. Rev 1 will fail — budget for it.

04 · share
Document the pain

A well-written README that explains the footguns is worth more than perfect code.
